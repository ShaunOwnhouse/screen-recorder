# Screen Recorder: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

Screen Recorder records your screen, and optionally your computer's sound and your microphone, only when you start a recording.

## What the app accesses

- **Screen:** only the screen or area you choose, and only while a recording is running.
- **Microphone:** only if you turn the microphone on, and only while recording or when you press *Test*.
- **Computer sound:** only if you turn it on, and only while recording or testing.

## What happens to your recordings

Recordings are stored only on your own computer: first in the app's local folder while you review them, then in the folder you choose when you save. You can discard any recording before it's saved.

## What the app does not do

- It does not upload recordings, audio or screenshots anywhere.
- It does not collect personal information, analytics or usage data.
- It does not contain advertising or third-party tracking.
- It does not share any data with anyone.

Questions about this policy: contact the publisher through the Microsoft Store listing.
