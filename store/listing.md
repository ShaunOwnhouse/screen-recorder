## Short description

Record your screen in full HD or 4K with crystal-clear quality, system sound and microphone, then trim and crop before saving.

## Description

Screen Recorder captures your screen at its true resolution — 1080p, 1440p or 4K — and keeps every pixel sharp in the saved video. Text stays crisp and motion stays smooth, so recordings look exactly like your screen.

• Record a whole screen or drag to select any area, on any connected monitor
• Near-lossless quality using your graphics card (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel), up to 120 fps
• Record computer sound and your microphone or headset, each with its own volume
• 3-2-1 countdown before recording starts
• The app stays out of your recordings: only what you're capturing ends up in the video
• Review every recording before it's saved: trim, cut out parts, crop, then save or discard
• Saves standard MP4 files that play everywhere — Windows Media Player, phones, WhatsApp and the web
• Global hotkey (Ctrl+Shift+R) to start and stop from anywhere

## Restricted capability justification (runFullTrust)

Screen Recorder is a desktop (Win32) application packaged with MSIX. It needs runFullTrust to capture the screen with the Windows Desktop Duplication API, to run its bundled FFmpeg encoder as a child process for hardware-accelerated video encoding, and to register a global hotkey for starting and stopping recordings. It does not access any data beyond what the user chooses to record.
